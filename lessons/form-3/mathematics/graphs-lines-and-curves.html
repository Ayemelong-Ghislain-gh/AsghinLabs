Straight-Line and Quadratic Graph Plotter

Move the sliders to see the graph change, then practise drawing a graph from a table of values.

🔭 Explore: move the sliders

    📘 Learn it: how to draw and read a graph

    Four short cards. Read them, then try it yourself below.

    ✍️ Try it yourself: from table to graph

    Fill in the table. Your points appear on the small graph. Wrong answers turn red with a hint.

    Try these

    • Set c = 3 and move m. What stays the same? (The line always crosses the y-axis at 3.)
    • Make m negative. Which way does the line lean?
    • Switch to the curve and change a from positive to negative. What happens to the shape?
    • Set a = 1, b = 0, c = −4. Where does the curve cross the x-axis? Those two points are the roots of x² − 4 = 0.
