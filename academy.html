How do I order a workbook?

Add the workbooks you want to your order, tap “Review order”, fill in your name and number, then send. It opens WhatsApp with everything written for you. We reply to confirm.

How do I pay?

You don't pay on this website. Once we confirm your order on WhatsApp, we agree the payment method and delivery or pickup with you.

Can I order for a whole class or school?

Yes. For 10 copies or more, ask for a group price and we'll prepare a quote for your class or school.

Are the notes really free?

Yes. You can read them online on any phone or computer, or download them to keep. New notes are added regularly.

Do you teach online?

Yes — private tutoring, exam preparation and tech skills classes are available in person or online, one-on-one or in small groups.
