Expanding Brackets

Expand double brackets with the grid method, one step at a time. You fill in each step and get corrected straight away.

✍️ Practise: expand step by step

New to this? Read the Learn it cards below first. Then work through the steps below. Type a number in each box (use a minus sign for negatives), press Check, and you'll be told what is right and why. Stuck? Press Show me.

📘 Learn it: why the grid method works

Stuck on a step? Go through these cards. Each one explains one idea with an example.

The grid method

  • Put the terms of the first bracket down the side and the terms of the second bracket across the top.
  • Multiply to fill each box. Mind the signs: negative × negative = positive.
  • Add the four boxes, then collect like terms (the two x boxes combine).
  • You can always check by picking a value, say x = 1: both the original and your answer must give the same number.
