Frequently asked questions

How much does a flyer design cost?

Flyer design starts at 5 000 FCFA. Pamphlets, posters, banners and bigger jobs are priced after we see what you need, and the price is agreed before we start.

Do you print the flyers too?

Yes, on request. Tell us the quantity, size and paper, and we arrange the printing. Printing is quoted separately from the design.

What do I need to send?

The purpose of the flyer, the text (or the main points), your logo, any photos and your contact details. If you have no logo yet, we can design one.

Can I use the same design on WhatsApp and Facebook?

Yes. We give you a version sized for WhatsApp status and social media as well as the print file.
