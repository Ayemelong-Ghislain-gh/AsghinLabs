❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you reach out
How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short chat about your goals, we send a clear quote with the scope and price agreed before any work starts. Use the budget field in the form below to give us a starting point.

How long does a project take?

Simple websites are usually quicker than custom software or AI tools. We give you a timeline together with the quote, so you know what to expect from day one.

Do you work with clients outside Cameroon?

Yes. We work remotely, so we can plan, build and hand over your project wherever you are.

What happens after my project is launched?

We test, deploy and hand it over, show you how to use it, and offer optional maintenance and improvements.

Where do I buy workbooks or join a class?

Everything for students is on AsghinLabs Academy — order workbooks, read free notes, or apply for GCE Computer Science coaching.
