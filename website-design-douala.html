Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Cameroon?

Our websites start at 75 000 FCFA for a simple 1–3 page site. Bigger sites, online shops and web apps are priced after a short chat, and the price is agreed before work starts. Our website cost guide explains what changes the price.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly we receive your texts and photos. We agree on a delivery date with you before we start.

Do you only work in Douala?

No. We are based in Douala and work with clients anywhere in Cameroon and abroad, over WhatsApp, phone and video calls.

Can customers order on WhatsApp from my website?

Yes. We add WhatsApp buttons, and for shops a cart that writes the full order and sends it to your WhatsApp.

Will my website appear on Google?

We build every site so Google can read it: clear titles, descriptions, fast pages and a sitemap. We also guide you to set up your free Google Business Profile, which helps you appear on Google Maps.
