Frequently asked questions

Do I need a bank account or card payments?

No. Most Cameroonian customers pay by MTN MoMo or Orange Money. The shop shows your payment numbers and the order arrives on WhatsApp, where you confirm payment and delivery.

Can I add or change products myself?

Yes. We show you how to update prices, photos and products, or we can do it for you.

How much does an online shop cost?

It depends on the number of products and features. Simple websites start at 75 000 FCFA; we give you a clear quote for your shop after a short chat on WhatsApp.

Can I share my products on Facebook and TikTok?

Yes. Every product and category has its own link you can post on Facebook, TikTok, Instagram or your WhatsApp Status.
