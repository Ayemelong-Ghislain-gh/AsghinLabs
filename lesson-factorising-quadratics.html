Factorising Quadratics

Factorising is expanding in reverse. Find the pair of numbers that multiplies and adds correctly, and the brackets follow.

The idea

  • To factorise x² + bx + c, find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b. Those numbers go in the brackets: (x + one)(x + other).
  • When there is a number in front of x² (ax² + bx + c), multiply a × c first. Find two numbers that multiply to a × c and add to b, use them to split the middle term, then group.
  • Always check by expanding the brackets. You must get back the original expression.
